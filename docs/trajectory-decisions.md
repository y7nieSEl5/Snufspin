# Ball tracking, calibration, and 3D trajectory decisions

Updated November 20, 2026. Consolidates the [3D reconstruction discussion](codex://threads/01a1ec89-b7f7-7020-96c7-fce9b3ab7482) and checks implementation status against `main` at `2c27631`. The [README](../README.md) has setup and commands. [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md) records upstream code and checkpoint provenance.

## Scope and decisions in force

- **Product.** Build offline, SwingVision-like analysis from a single fixed recreational camera, including tracking, speed, landing, shot type and dead-time removal. Low behind-baseline footage is an important benchmark, with side and oblique views also in scope. Prioritize downloadable footage, released weights and small targeted reviews given recording and hardware constraints.
- **Ball observations.** Keep official WASB Tennis at stride 1 as the default. Use raw measured detections or manual centers as flight-fit evidence and preserve the provenance of inferred points.
- **Court geometry.** Default to TEXflip LETR, vanishing-direction grouping, and direct segment fitting with GeoCalib intrinsics. Keep the crossing solver, including its guarded Farin fallback, available for comparison and manual landmarks. [Current calibration architecture](architecture.md#3-court-and-camera-calibration)
- **Airborne reconstruction.** Start with one manually bounded flight, a checked full camera and fixed gravity. Evaluate on the same shots with manual ball centers and raw WASB. Use the video-estimated camera for automatic runs and the published CalTennis camera as a benchmark control to isolate flight fitting from calibration error.
- **Use 3D where it adds information.** Metric flight helps with average speed, net clearance, apex and short occlusions. A visible bounce can be mapped directly to the metric court plane. Swing cues and event timing serve forehand/backhand, volleys and dead-time removal. Flight and bounce features can supplement shot and spin classification.

## 2D tracking and candidate selection

| Work | Result and decision |
| --- | --- |
| **TrackNet baseline** | The legacy pipeline followed [tennis-vision](https://github.com/philippdubach/tennis-vision), with TennisProject's `BallTrackerNet` and the [yastrebksv TrackNet weights](https://github.com/yastrebksv/TrackNet). The accepted tag `tracking-baseline-v1` includes timestamp-based linear interpolation across gaps with endpoint separation up to 0.15 s. It helped short hardcourt gaps and sometimes propagated bad endpoints or filled hidden frames. After startup, 25 of 26 reviewed visible misses lacked a Hough-circle candidate. The connected-component/velocity probe rescued a few frames, still followed the hand and used frozen history. Keep it as diagnostic evidence. See the [historical review](../benchmarks/tracking/README.md). |
| **Paired WASB comparison** | The six GridTrackNet excerpts showed broadly even performance between WASB and interpolated TrackNet under visual review, with some complementary wins and hand latches in both. Keep WASB as the current default without inferring a general accuracy gain from this sample. Reuse official 512×288 preprocessing, weighted connected components and overlapping three-frame windows. The pinned selector takes the highest-score candidate inside the displacement gate. The velocity/acceleration predictor described in the paper is inactive in that implementation. [WASB paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05237) · [Pinned implementation](https://github.com/nttcom/WASB-SBDT/blob/923462cacdeb3353b84ddebdedb3f4b7a8553b0f/src/trackers/online.py) |
| **Grass hand-latch diagnostic** | Log the winner, surviving alternatives and gate rejects. In frames 25–39, frame 35 was the only reviewed failure with a decoded ball candidate within 5 source px. Two candidates passed the gate there, the closest 0.6 px from the label. The hand-area winner was 32.6 px away. Visible errors at frames 28, 29 and 33 lacked a usable candidate near the ball. This can justify rejecting an unreliable observation, while recovery requires stronger image evidence or a separately marked inference. |
| **CalTennis pilot and motion replay** | Three native-timing, roughly one-minute sessions were prepared and reviewed with candidate overlays. Camera angle was left flexible. The replay used two short robust image-space motion fits from frozen WASB winners, with an allowance for direction changes at hits and bounces. At an 11.25 source px tolerance, reject-only removed 512 positions and rerank removed 461. Visual review found many correct points removed and about one clear correction. The logs lacked ball labels for an accuracy score. Set both variants aside. Perspective curvature, self-contaminated motion fits and smooth wrist motion limit this rule. |

The comparison, grass diagnosis, replay and [CalTennis pilot](https://huggingface.co/datasets/demalenk/caltennis) protocol are in `wasb-candidate-diagnostics` at `1eefbb2`, under `benchmarks/tracking/` (`wasb-gridtracknet.md`, `wasb-grass-candidates.md`, `wasb-motion-replay.md` and `caltennis/`). Local review media live under `outputs/`.

**Next tracking experiment, still open.** Label 10–20 visible in-flight misses and inspect raw WASB heatmaps near the ball. Try a local decoder threshold for weak peaks and manually centered higher-resolution crops when the peak is absent. Consider automatic cropping or tiling only after a consistent gain. Keep ordinary rallies, visible contacts and tosses as controls. Count independent latch episodes and distinguish visible, occluded, out-of-frame and uncertain frames.

**Deferred tracking references.** [Yan et al.](https://storage.googleapis.com/lacuna-pdfs-lunar-airport-385023/pdfs/16/51/165146f2d760226b8869d3ba501ae6315f25a0380f19f24a437e4669202dd9bb.pdf) use later tracklets to resolve clutter, including wristbands. [Zhou et al.](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/51400/) use multiple paths and player regions. [CourtVision](https://github.com/kzheng18/court-vision) is a reference for observation provenance, quadratic jump rejection and forward/backward smoothing. [WASB-SBDT-FPFilter](https://github.com/ZSHYC/WASB-SBDT-FPFilter) scores crops of selected detections, with separate classifier weights unverified in the survey. WASB's own predictor, multi-path selection and a soft RTMPose wrist co-motion penalty remain untested here. Mere wrist proximity would penalize genuine contacts and tosses. Racket tracking was deferred to event and contact work.

## Court calibration

| Work | Result and decision |
| --- | --- |
| **TennisCourtDetector** | The broadcast-trained 14-landmark heatmap model remains an optional backend. It had at most two usable landmarks per sampled CalTennis frame and yielded no paint-supported homography on the three amateur clips. Comparing the upstream Hough-circle decoder with Snufspin's blob centroid put only 4 of 48 reviewed amateur points within 12 px for either decoder, all on grass. A hardcourt heatmap peaked 305 px from the manual click. The failure already existed before decoding, so switching decoders alone was set aside. [Upstream model](https://github.com/yastrebksv/TennisCourtDetector) |
| **TEXflip / LETR** | Adopted pretrained tennis line detection and metric court-template fitting. Long interior line segments give evidence when outer corners are cropped. The amateur grass and clay fits passed. Hardcourt's per-frame fits disagreed. Parallel lines in the world can converge in the image, so a single projected template and consistent line families are the relevant geometric checks. [TEXflip](https://github.com/TEXflip/tennis-court-detection) |
| **Farin paint scoring** | Reused cached LETR segments and scored fixed-view template hypotheses across seven frames per clip against visible paint in both court directions. Shared selection recovered hardcourt at 3.0 px median and 6.1 px p90 against review clicks. Paint-only selection was ambiguous on grass. The refinement experiment made no accepted change on hardcourt and worsened typical clay landmark error while slightly improving the tail. Keep refinement in the benchmark. On `main`, `--paint-fallback` opts in to guarded shared selection after frame-fit disagreement. A tied result stays unresolved. Test on unseen clips before making it automatic. [Farin et al.](https://www.dirk-farin.net/publications/data/Farin2004b.pdf) |
| **CalTennis supplied cameras** | Use the three supplied cameras as clip-specific references. CalTennis uses iPhone metadata for intrinsics and court intersections with PnP for pose. Its JSONs specify 1920×1080 and the decoded MP4s are 1920×1088. A half-pixel-aware vertical resize of intrinsics is the working correction. Landmark checks supported a vertical adjustment, with a roughly four-pixel translation also scoring well. The original encoding transform remains unverified. The Mar 4 oblique view is the first flight test reference. Camera sidecars bind to the exact video, so reimport against the excerpt when fitting an excerpt. [CalTennis calibration](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20542v1#S4) |
| **Court-only intrinsics** | Known court dimensions set the plane scale. With square pixels, centered principal point and negligible distortion, two orthogonal vanishing directions can constrain focal length. With reviewed landmarks, two oblique CalTennis views came within 1.4–2.3% of reference focal length. The north view stayed plane-only. All three amateur GridTrackNet clips have a usable plane when the hardcourt fallback is included, and none passed the full camera checks. Near-parallel image lines can make focal length unstable despite a good plane overlay. |

The calibration checks, reference sidecars and comparison scripts are in `texflip-court-detection` at `3da218b`, under `benchmarks/calibration/` (`README.md`, `texflip.md`, `farin-paint.md` and `references/`). The [CLI documentation](../README.md#fixed-camera-court-calibration) describes the merged behavior. The metric court surface defines `z = 0`. Its homography maps a surface pixel to court coordinates. A tracked ball center at bounce is about one radius above that plane. Airborne observations require the full camera for viewing rays and a flight model to estimate depth along them.

**Next calibration experiment.** Evaluate the default segment solver on unseen fixed views. The CalTennis benchmark on `caltennis-blind-calibration` compared the crossing solver, GeoCalib and AnyCalib with published cameras held out for scoring. The default now estimates intrinsics with GeoCalib across the sampled frames. AnyCalib remains a comparison for unknown crop or resize. Compare focal length, camera center, world-ray angle and paint alignment where reference measurements are available. Sweep focal length and refit pose to see how sharply the court constrains the solution. Try a measured net center or post top as an elevated constraint, borrowing [TT3D's focal-length/pose fitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10035) and [TVCalib's segment fitting](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/papers/Theiner_TVCalib_Camera_Calibration_for_Sports_Field_Registration_in_Soccer_WACV_2023_paper.pdf). Test [straight-line distortion correction](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2014/106/) if wide-angle footage shows bowed lines. [Agrawal et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.06977) (near-half fitting using the net) and [CourtAlign-2S](https://openreview.net/pdf/01b2e7445170ebd4328ed615e1196d4ce6b880ef.pdf) (broadcast segmentation, repo unavailable during the survey) remain alternate court detectors.

## 3D reconstruction

**Implemented.** `8c762d7` added [`fit-flight`](../README.md#gravity-only-flight-fit). It uses real timestamps, a calibrated-ray seed and robust source-pixel reprojection fitting at fixed gravity over one selected airborne interval. It accepts manual and measured observations and returns inliers, diagnostics and a `MetricTrack` over the supported interval. The local Mar 4 CalTennis smoke test accepted frames 2966–3021 with 56 inliers and 0.98 px median reprojection error. Ten withheld observations gave 0.90 px median error. These are image-space checks. The local files are under `outputs/flight-smoke/`. Independent real-video speed and 3D accuracy remain unmeasured.

| Reference | Decision or possible use |
| --- | --- |
| [TT3D](https://github.com/cogsys-tuebingen/tt3d) | Primary physics-fitting reference. MIT-licensed table-tennis code optimizes reprojection around a bounce. Borrow the idea with lawn-tennis geometry and dynamics. A bounce-height constraint is still pending in Snufspin's gravity-only fitter. |
| [Ball3D](https://lukaszgrad.github.io/soccer-ball3d/) | Borrow the evaluation design with triangulated truth. Simpler flight models performed better on its monocular soccer benchmark, while richer models benefited from 3D observations. The CC BY-NC-SA code is a research reference. Compare fixed drag after gravity has been evaluated, before fitting free spin. |
| [Tennis-Vision](https://github.com/HarshTomar1234/Tennis-Vision) and [Vid2Player](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.04524) | Untested endpoint baselines. Tennis-Vision uses estimated endpoints and event times for a gravity arc. Vid2Player uses annotated hits and player-derived contact positions to constrain plausible flights. Try a soft contact prior from tracked player location, [tennis-vision RTMPose](https://github.com/philippdubach/tennis-vision) or [RacketVision](https://github.com/OrcustD/RacketVision) around contact. |
| [Where Is The Ball](https://where-is-the-ball.github.io/) | Main learned-height reference for later full-rally work. It encodes rays by intersection with ground and vertical planes, predicts event boundaries and height with temporal networks and refines the lifted 3D points. Code, synthetic data and weights were unavailable during the survey. Its ground-contact endpoints and synthetic physics without aerodynamics need adapting. The reported real landing comparison used projected bounce labels and leaves airborne depth/speed unverified. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05763v1) |
| [SynthNet](https://stellagrasshof.com/assets/pdf/2024_ertner_mmisport.pdf) | Tennis-specific learned alternative using WASB, event cues and a synthetic-trained estimate of the initial 3D flight state. Reported difficulty on a low view and far-side depth. Public checkpoint unverified. Compare per-shot state prediction with sequence-height prediction if we train. |
| [Uplifting Table Tennis](https://github.com/KieDani/UpliftingTableTennis) | Released GPL-3.0 implementation, weights, synthetic data and MuJoCo generator. Use as a training/inference reference. Lawn-tennis dynamics and camera geometry would require tennis-specific training. |
| [Australian Open trajectory decomposition](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12283-026-00547-6) and [public Hawk-Eye-derived CourtVision data](https://github.com/ryurko/hawkeye) | The study fits drag and spin to private Hawk-Eye flight curves. The public scrape has sparse hits, peaks, net crossings and bounces. Use those as possible priors or simulation sanity checks. Dense paired video and metric validation data are unavailable in the scrape. |

**Next flight benchmark.**

1. For the proposed pilot, mark contact and first-bounce times on 12–18 flights across the three CalTennis sessions, including clean flights, gaps and latches. Use Oct 2 and Mar 4 for development and hold out Mar 9. Run the same fitter with manual centers and raw WASB, keeping interpolated points out of the fit. Compare loss of support when timing moves by one frame, observations disappear or the same WASB track is fitted with an automatically estimated camera. Use synthetic trajectories with known 3D to test missing observations and clusters of false detections.
2. Compare visible-bounce court mapping with a TT3D-style fit that constrains the ball to one radius above the surface at the marked bounce time. Fit the two horizontal coordinates and three pre-bounce velocity components, then check that the arc stays above the court and descends into the bounce. Withhold the clicked bounce pixel when scoring estimated landing. Evaluate hidden-bounce prediction separately and withhold its event anchor for that test.
3. Find overlapping CalTennis cameras, verify timing at tennis events and triangulate reviewed ball centers for independent speed and airborne 3D checks. Published calibration and pose-based synchronization are useful starting points. At 30 m/s one frame at 60 fps corresponds to 0.5 m of travel.
4. Report supported-interval average flight speed before attempting racket-exit speed. Compare landing, metric 3D, speed error and the fraction of shots with a stable estimate. Surface unreliable metrics as unavailable. Use the existing [reconstruction hook](../snufspin/pipeline.py) to feed `MetricTrack` into shot analysis once benchmarked. The planned end-to-end output is a per-shot landing and speed report with a reprojection overlay that distinguishes measured points from inferred positions.
